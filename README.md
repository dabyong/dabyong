### 03 — NYC Taxi Demand Forecasting & Operational Planning
**Python · DuckDB · scikit-learn · Power BI**

Developed a reproducible, one-day-ahead pickup demand forecasting case study using historical New York City Yellow Taxi trip data.

- Evaluated zone-level forecasts against a **same-weekday-last-week baseline**.
- Achieved **10.20% zone-day WAPE** on the final holdout versus **16.73%** for the baseline.
- Created an operational Power BI report covering demand, accuracy, and planning watchlists.

*Offline historical evaluation; not a live forecasting service.*

[**Explore Repository →**](https://github.com/dabyong/nyc-taxi-demand-forecasting)

### 04 — AI-Powered Marketing Lead Prioritization
**Python · scikit-learn · Streamlit**

Built a research prototype that ranks historical bank-marketing leads for limited outreach capacity.

- Compared classification approaches and assessed ranking quality, calibration, and subgroup behavior.
- Selected a seven-feature Random Forest model after a fairness-sensitive feature ablation.
- Reached **1.89× Lift@10%** on the locked holdout, with documented limitations around temporal shift and uncalibrated scores.

*Research demonstration only; the live app accepts synthetic examples, not real customer data.*

[**Explore Repository →**](https://github.com/dabyong/marketing-lead-scoring) · [**Live Demo →**](https://marketing-lead-scoring-9nm4ut8erckcwkmptyz9rd.streamlit.app/)
